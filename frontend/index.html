Scientific Scope, Interpretation & Limitations

Research Caveats
•
Classification Model, Not Affinity Predictor: This system performs binary classification of cognate vs. mismatched complexes under the Zhang et al. (2024) Test-3 experimental paradigm. It does not predict physical binding affinity constants ($K_D$, $K_A$, or $\Delta G$).
•
Model Probability Is Not Calibrated Binding Probability: The reported probability represents the Sigmoid output of the CNN on the 20×21 intramolecular frequency tensor; it must not be interpreted as an experimental chance of physical binding.
•
Test-3 Performance Is Near Chance: As reported by Zhang et al. (2024), Test-3 cognate-vs-mismatched classification accuracy on held-out structural complexes is near chance (~50% accuracy, ~0.515 AUC). Intramolecular contact charts lack 3D spatial docking orientation, so mismatched pairs exhibit residue distributions similar to cognate complexes.
•
Decoy Negatives vs. Verified Non-Binders: Negative training examples represent synthetic non-cognate pairings (decoy combinations) rather than experimentally verified non-binding complexes.
•
Test 1 vs. Test 3 Distinction: Test 1 (Antibody–antigen vs. general protein–protein interactions) and Test 3 (Cognate vs. mismatched antibody–antigen pairing) are separate tasks with different baseline expectations.
